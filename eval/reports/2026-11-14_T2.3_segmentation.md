# T2.3 — Clause segmentation and doc_terms (2026-11-14)

> **Synthetic documents; simulated photos.** Five generated agreements (AG02, AG04, AG06, AG08, AG10 — chosen to cover footnotes, small print and an extra KFS row) with hand-labelled clause starts (`segmentation_gold.yaml`). Real lender documents have more varied layouts (multi-column, nested sub-clauses, multi-page tables), so these figures are optimistic and are not the HC-4 acceptance measurement.

Pipeline: Docling (reading order) → `segment.py` → lexicon pass → Gemma 4 31B structuring pass (`google.gemma-4-31b`, temperature 0, PII redacted) → doc 04 normaliser (`norm-1.0`, the only source of values).

## Findings

1. **Digital: boundary F1 1.000** after one fix found by this run. Docling's layout model merged clauses 2–6 and the
   signature line of AG04/AG08 into a single list item (first run: digital F1 0.892). The segmenter now splits a block
   where a sentence ends and a new number or signature line begins, and lets an un-numbered item continue a clause
   that ended mid-sentence. Pieces keep the merged block's bbox, so the evidence highlight covers the whole block.
2. **Photos: boundary F1 0.926.** Every miss is a line that OCR garbled on the simulated photo (same lower-page region
   in each case), not a segmentation error: the clause is found but its text is unreadable. All of those clauses have
   `ocr_conf` 0.58–0.62, below T_ocr 0.70, so the OCR gate (doc 08 §5) treats them as low and they cannot support a
   CONFLICT.
3. **doc_terms: 0 wrong values.** The lexicon pass alone finds 38/47 truth terms (all KFS-table terms); the Gemma pass
   adds the clause, footnote and small-print terms (47/47 digital). On photos the 2 misses are the garbled clauses;
   2 terms are unparsed (value=None), never guessed.
4. Every value comes from the normaliser; every LLM `value_raw` was verbatim (0 dropped across 10 calls).
