Consent to process your recording and documents

By continuing, you agree that SaidSigned may process the conversation recording and the loan documents you upload in this session, so that it can transcribe the conversation, find what was said about the loan, and compare it with your documents.

- Your recording is sent to a speech-to-text service to be transcribed. Parts of the transcript and documents may be sent to an AI service to extract loan terms.
- SaidSigned shows possible mismatches and questions to ask. It does not give legal or financial advice and does not say that anyone acted wrongly.
- Your data is kept for a limited period and you can withdraw this consent at any time. Withdrawing stops all processing for this session.
